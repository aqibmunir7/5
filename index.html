Our story

Eleven years in Paris,
now on College Street

Belleville is run by Shelley MacDonald, who spent 11 years in Paris, working and studying at Le Cordon Bleu.

She started Belleville with weekly pickups from her apartment, and now runs the bakery on College Street in Burlington — French technique, elevated comfort food, baked in small batches.

If you have only seen Belleville on Instagram, this page is the missing piece: the address, the hours, and how to preorder before the counter sells out.
